# Contact BizzKitt BV

For product questions and support, email **hello@bizzkitt.ai**. BizzKitt BV is based in the Netherlands.

## Product questions

Include the product name and the workflow you need to check. We can clarify the documented features, tested compatibility and limitations. Check the [product overview](README.md) for the current availability of each tool. A documentation page is not a purchase or download page.

## Report a problem

Include your product version, IDE/Unity/WooCommerce version as applicable, operating system, expected result, observed result and the smallest fictional example that reproduces the issue.

Do not send passwords, API keys, license keys, payment details, personal customer records or a complete confidential project. Remove private paths and sensitive values from logs and reports before sharing them.

Support covers the product's documented behaviour and tested configurations. No response-time guarantee, custom implementation service or compatibility with untested versions is advertised.

## Purchase and licensing questions

No payments are taken through this repository. When a product is approved and available, its official marketplace listing will describe its price, license and applicable purchase terms. The [product overview](README.md) states which products are still awaiting release.
